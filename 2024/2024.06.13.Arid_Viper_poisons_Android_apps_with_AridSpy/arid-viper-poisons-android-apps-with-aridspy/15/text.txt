Conclusion
Five campaigns, most likely operated by the Arid Viper APT group, distribute Android spyware, which we've
named AridSpy, via dedicated websites, with AridSpy’s malicious code implanted into various trojanized apps.
This malware family has two additional stages that are downloaded from a C&C server. The purpose of the
second-stage payload is espionage via victim data exfiltration. AridSpy also has a hardcoded internal version
number that differs in these five campaigns and from other samples disclosed before. This information suggests
that AridSpy is maintained and might receive updates or functionality changes.
