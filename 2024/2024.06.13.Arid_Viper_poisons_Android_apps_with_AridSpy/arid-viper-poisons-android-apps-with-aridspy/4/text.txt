Victimology
Altogether we detected six occurrences of AridSpy in our telemetry, from Palestine and Egypt. The majority of the
spyware instances registered in Palestine were for the malicious Palestinian Civil Registry app, with one other
detection not being part of any campaign mentioned in this blogpost. We then found the same first-stage payload
but with a different package name in Egypt. There was also another first-stage payload detected in Egypt, one that
uses the same C&C servers as the samples in the LapizaChat and job opportunity campaigns.
