First-stage payload
During installation of the malicious update, the first-stage payload displays app names such as Play
Manager or Service Google . This payload works separately, without the necessity of having the trojanized
app installed on the same device. This means that if the victim uninstalls the initial trojanized app, for example
LapizaChat, AridSpy will not be in any way affected.
Functionality-wise, the first-stage payload is similar to the trojanized application. It is responsible for downloading
the second-stage payload, which is then dynamically loaded and executed. The first-stage payload downloads an
AES-encrypted second-stage payload from a hardcoded URL and controls its further execution.
