SQLULDR2
SQLULDR2 is a command-line utility written in C/C++ that can be used to export the contents of a
remote Oracle database to a local text-based file. There are multiple command-line parameters available
to specify the details of the data export including but not limited to: query, user, rows, and text.
APT41 exported data from Oracle Databases to CSV formats with the following command:
C:\ProgramData\luldr\luldr\sqluldr.exe user=@:1521/
 charset=utf8 safe=yes head=yes text=csv rows=50000000
batch=yes query= file=.csv
