Campaign 2: 2019 Poison Carp Attack
In September 2019, Citizen Lab reported on attackers targeting Tibetan groups via multiple iOS and
Android exploits. Citizen Lab attributed the attack to the Chinese threat group they track as POISON
CARP. The report references domains that were tied to an IP address that we found references to in
this data leak.
The IP address 74.120.172[.]10 was associated with the domain mailteso[.]online
between Sept. 22, 2020, and Feb. 20, 2024, and mailnotes[.]online between Aug. 7, 2021, and
July 12, 2022.
