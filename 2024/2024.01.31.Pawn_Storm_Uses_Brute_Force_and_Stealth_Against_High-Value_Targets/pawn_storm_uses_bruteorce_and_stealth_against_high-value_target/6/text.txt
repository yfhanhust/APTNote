Pawn Storm, which has been active since at least 2004, began focusing
more on operational security during the past few years, with the group’s
TTPs changing slowly over time.
One of the most common methods used by Pawn Storm to break into
systems is brute force credential attacks. Since 2019, the actor has been
actively trying to brute force its way into mail servers and the corporate
virtual private network (VPN) services of organizations around the world. ​
We believe that Pawn Storm has had success with its campaigns and we
assess that the threat actor has managed to breach thousands of email
addresses, which we’ve observed are being abused to send additional
waves of spear phishing emails (most likely for information gathering, but
also to be used as infrastructure for other attacks).
