### Overview of North Korean Cyber Actors and Their Operations

#### DarkSeoul (2013)

**Summary:**
- **Incident:** On March 20, 2013, a coordinated cyber attack known as "DarkSeoul" targeted South Korean banks and media organizations.
- **Impact:** The attack caused significant disruptions, including the deletion of data from hard drives and the temporary shutdown of services.

**Known Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs):**
- **Wiper Malware:** The malware used in this attack was designed to wipe data from infected systems, causing irreparable damage.
- **DDoS Attacks:** Distributed Denial of Service attacks were employed to overwhelm targeted networks.
- **Targets:**
  - **Financial Sector:** Banks and financial institutions.
  - **Media Organizations:** Television stations and news outlets.

**Associated Actors:**
- **WhoIs Team:** Claimed responsibility for an attack on the LG +U website, which was part of a broader campaign.
- **New Romantic Cyber Army Team (NRAT):** Also claimed responsibility for the March 2013 attacks.
- **Hastati:** Another group that used Roman and classical terms in their code.

#### IsOne

**Summary:**
- **Incident:** In June 2012, the website of South Korean newspaper JoongAng Ilbo was defaced and an attempt was made to wipe the servers.
- **Impact:** The attack resulted in temporary downtime and defacement but did not succeed in wiping the servers.

**Known TTPs:**
- **Wiper Malware:** Attempted to delete data from the server.
- **Defacements:** Altered the website content with a laughing cat image.
- **Targets:**
  - **JoongAng Ilbo:** A prominent South Korean newspaper.

**Investigation Findings:**
- The attack shared characteristics with previous North Korean cyber operations, including the use of servers in multiple countries and connections to North Korean telecommunications companies.
