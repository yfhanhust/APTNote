During the last weeks, various samples of Uroburos (also named Urob, Turla, Sengoku, Snark and Pfinet ) were
analyzed and reports have been published [1][234], also analyses about a suspected predecessor, Agent.btz, are public
5 . CIRCL analyzed an older version of Turla, known as a representative of the Pfinet malware family. The objective
of this analysis is to gather additional Indicators of Compromise or behaviors in order to improve detection and to
discover additional insights into the malware. This document is not considered a final release but a work-inprogress document.
