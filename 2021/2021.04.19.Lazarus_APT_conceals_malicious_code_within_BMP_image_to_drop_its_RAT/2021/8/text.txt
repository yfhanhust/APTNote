Conclusion
The Lazarus threat actor is one of the most active and sophisticated North Korean threat
actors that has targeted several countries including South Korea, the U.S. and Japan in
the past couple of years. The group is known to develop custom malware families and use
new techniques in its operations. In this blog we documented a spear phishing attack
operated by this APT group that has targeted South Korea.
The actor has used a clever method to bypass security mechanisms in which it has
embedded its malicious HTA file as a compressed zlib file within a PNG file that then has
been decompressed during run time by converting itself to the BMP format. The dropped
payload was a loader that decoded and decrypted the second stage payload into memory.
The second stage payload has the capability to receive and execute commands/shellcode
as well as perform exfiltration and communications to a command and control server.
