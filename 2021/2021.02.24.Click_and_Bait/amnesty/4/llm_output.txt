The spyware identified by the Security Lab were either for Mac OS or Windows systems.
The Windows spyware was a variant of a malware family called Kerrdown and used
exclusively by the Ocean Lotus group. Kerrdown is a downloader that installs additional

downloaded Cobalt Strike, a commercial spyware toolkit developed by the American
company Strategy Cyber and routinely used to lawfully audit the security of organizations

including executing scripts, taking screenshots or logging keystrokes. Unlicensed versions
of Cobalt Strikes have been increasingly used by attack groups, including Ocean Lotus,
