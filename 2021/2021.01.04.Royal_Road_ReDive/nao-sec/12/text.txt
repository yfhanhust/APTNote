In the previous blog, we summarized the characteristics of attack groups that use Royal Road.
We used it to divide the attack groups into two groups. However, by 2020, those
characteristics are almost meaningless. It has been standardized or deleted. It’s not as easy to
group as it used to be. In the first place, the groups sharing Royal Road should be close. We do
not classify further, but if you have any comments please let us know.
