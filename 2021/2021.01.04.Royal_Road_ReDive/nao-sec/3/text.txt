Abstract
We introduced the “Royal Road RTF Weaponizer” in our previous blog [1] (and the presented
at Japan Security Analyst Conference 2020 and CPX 360 CPRCon 2020). Royal Road is a tool
shared by many targeted attack groups believed to belong to China. It’s been a year since our
previous blog, and Royal Road is still in use. Here, we will introduce the Royal Road-related
attacks observed during 2020.
