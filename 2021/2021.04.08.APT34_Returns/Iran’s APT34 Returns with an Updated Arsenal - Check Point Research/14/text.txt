Conclusion
Iran backed APT34 shows no sign of slowing down, further pushing its political agenda in
the middle-east, with an ongoing focus on Lebanon – using offensive cyber operations.
While maintaining its modus operandi and reusing old techniques, as reviewed above, the
group continues to create new and updated tools to minimize the possible detection of
their tools by security vendors.
In this publication we analyzed the newest backdoor variant deployed by the group’s
ongoing job opportunities campaigns, which includes malicious documents with job
offers – a technique they have successfully employed since at least 2018.
Check Point Sandblast protects against this APT attack, and prevents it from the very first
steps.
