Development timelines
The newest Hornbill sample was identified by Lookout’s app analysis engine as recently
as December 2020, suggesting the malware may still be active today. Both ChatSpy and
Hornbill’s packaging dates appeared to have been tampered with, but we first observed
them in January 2018 and May 2018 respectively.
Lookout first observed SunBird in January 2017, but unlike the other two malware
families, the packaging dates appear legitimate, indicating the malware was likely in
development between December 2016 and early 2019.
Hornbill, which Lookout first saw in May 2018, is actively deployed. We observed new samples as
recently as December 2020. The first SunBird sample was seen as early as 2017 and as late as
December 2019.
