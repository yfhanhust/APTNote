The campaign involves a two-step attack. During the first phase, an email without a malicious payload
containing content copied from a legitimate Pakistani newspaper’s article is sent to the target. The sender
address, which is spoofed, impersonates the PR wing of the Pakistani Armed Forces (info@ispr.gov.pk).
Two days later, a second email — purportedly a warning from a Pakistani military about the Pegasus
spyware — containing a cutt.ly link to a malicious encrypted Word document and the password for
decryption will be sent to the target. The sender address impersonates a service similar to that on the first
email (alert@ispr.gov.pk).
