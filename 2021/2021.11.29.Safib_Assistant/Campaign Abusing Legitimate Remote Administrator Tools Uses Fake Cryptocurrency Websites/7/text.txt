Associated malware
SpyAgent usually downloads other malware to perform additional tasks such as stealing
important data.
We noticed using SpyAgent downloading the following commodity stealers:
RedLine Stealer
Ducky stealer
AZOrult
Cypress Stealer
Clipper (a clipboard replacer that replaces various cryptocurrency addresses with
those controlled by the malicious actor)
We also noticed other RATS being used in the campaign, such as:
Remcos RAT
NanoCore
njRAT
AsyncRAT
