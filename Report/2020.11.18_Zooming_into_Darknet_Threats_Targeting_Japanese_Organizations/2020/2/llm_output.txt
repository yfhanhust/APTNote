While personal information and internal documents require malicious
actors to perform multiple steps to attack the company further (for
example, via specially-crafted phishing campaigns), exposed credentials
provide an easier way to compromise the company via accessing its internal
systems and software in use.
Exposed credentials can be divided into two types:
Leaked credentials. Corporate email logins, with or without passwords, which

emails in our sources.
