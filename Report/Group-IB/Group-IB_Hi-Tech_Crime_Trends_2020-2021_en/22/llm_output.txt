**Tortoiseshell**

**OilRig**

- **Objective**: Steal sensitive data and intellectual property.

### Resurgence of Dormant Groups

#### Tortoiseshell
- **Target**: Saudi Arabia
- **Objective**: Gain access to critical infrastructure and sensitive data.
- **Methods**: Used a previously unknown backdoor called "FROGSWITCH" and targeted an IT service provider.

### Notable Cyber Operations

#### Attacks on Nuclear Facilities

**India, 2019**
- **Incident**: North Korean group Lazarus compromised the Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL).
- **Impact**: Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant was affected; one unit shut down due to low steam generator levels.
- **Response**: NPCIL confirmed a malware infection in an administrative network but denied critical internal systems were compromised.

**South Korea, 2020**
- **Incident**: Lazarus sent malicious emails to a South Korean energy company.
- **Target**: KHNP (Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power Co., Ltd.)
- **Methods**: Used a document disguised as a job vacancy description.
- **Impact**: Increased attacks on South Korea's defense enterprises since April 2020.

**Iran, 2020**
- **Incident**: Suspected Israeli cyberattack on Iran's Natanz uranium-enrichment facility.
- **Impact**: Caused a fire and explosion; significant damage to advanced centrifuges but no casualties.
- **Claim**: A group called Homeland Cheetahs claimed responsibility.

### Attacks on Critical Infrastructure

#### Israel's Water Supply Facilities
- **Incident**: Cyberattacks on SCADA systems of wastewater treatment facilities in April 2020.
- **Impact**: Attempted to alter water chlorine levels, which could have poisoned the local population if successful.
- **Response**: Attacks thwarted; INCD issued security alerts.

#### Iran’s Critical Facilities
- **Incident**: Series of explosions and power outages, including a large explosion near Tehran in June 2020.
- **Impact**: Damage to Khodjir missile production facility.
- **Claim**: Iranian officials linked the attack to a power outage in Shiraz.
- **Response**: American and Israeli intelligence denied involvement.

### Analysis of Cyber Operations

**India: NPCIL Attack**
- **Key Points**: Lazarus group's long-term presence, successful exploitation of vulnerabilities, and potential for significant operational disruption.

**South Korea: Energy Sector Attack**
- **Key Points**: Targeted recruitment process to gain access, increased focus on defense enterprises, and use of sophisticated malware.

**Iran: Natanz Facility Attack**
- **Key Points**: Suspected state-sponsored attack, significant damage to nuclear infrastructure, and potential geopolitical implications.

**Israel: Water Supply Attacks**
- **Key Points**: SCADA system compromise, attempted poisoning, and successful mitigation by authorities.

**Iran: Critical Facilities Explosions**
- **Key Points**: Multiple incidents, suspected cyber component, and denial of involvement by major powers.

### Conclusion
The resurgence of dormant APT groups and the increasing sophistication of cyber operations highlight the ongoing threat to critical infrastructure. These incidents underscore the need for robust cybersecurity measures and continuous monitoring to prevent and mitigate potential attacks.
