APT trends report Q2 2019

By GReAT
For two years, the Global Research and Analysis Team (GReAT) at Kaspersky has been
publishing quarterly summaries of advanced persistent threat (APT) activity. The summaries are
based on our threat intelligence research and provide a representative snapshot of what we have
published and discussed in greater detail in our private APT reports. They aim to highlight the
significant events and findings that we feel people should be aware of.
This is our latest installment, focusing on activities that we observed during Q2 2019.
Readers who would like to learn more about our intelligence reports or request more information
on a specific report are encouraged to contact ‘intelreports@kaspersky.com’.
