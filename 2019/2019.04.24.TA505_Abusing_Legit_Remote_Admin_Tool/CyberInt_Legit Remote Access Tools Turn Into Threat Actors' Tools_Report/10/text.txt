BROADER USE OF RMS
Numerous Russian-language forum and social media posts, along with YouTube video tutorials, detail how
to package the legitimate RMS components for use in malicious campaigns and appear to date back to at
least 2011.
Threat actors such as TA505 have likely
evolved their use of RMS over time to the
point where they now package and deliver
the capable tool with their own malware;
a blend of proprietary threats with off-the
shelf components combined with tried
and tested TTP.

That being said, the accessibility and simplicity of using RMS for nefarious purposes likely explains the
prevalence of the tool in unrelated malicious campaigns, many of which share similar TTP but differ in their
configuration and use of RMS.
