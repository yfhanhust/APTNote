|Description|(CrowdStrike) Winnti Group, Blackfly, Wicked Panda refers to the targeted intrusionoperations of the actor publicly known as “Winnti,” whereas Wicked Spiderrepresents this group’s financially-motivated criminal activity. Originally, WickedSpider was observed exploiting a number of gaming companies and stealing code-signing certificates for use in other operations associated with the malware knownas Winnti. Now, Winnti is commonly associated with the interests of the governmentof the People’s Republic of China (PRC).Wicked Spider has been observed targeting technology companies in Germany,Indonesia, the Russian Federation, South Korea, Sweden, Thailand, Turkey, theUnited States, and elsewhere. Notably, Wicked Spider has often targeted gamingcompanies for their certificates, which can be used in future PRC-based operationsto sign malware. Ongoing analysis is still evaluating how these certificates are used— whether Wicked Spider hands the certificates off to other adversaries for use infuture campaigns or stockpiles them for its own use.|
