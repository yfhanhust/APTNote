In the fantasy book “A Court of Thorns and Roses” by Sarah J. Maas, Attor was an evil Faerie. In that book, the Faeries ruled over all the known world and humans were their slaves. In cybersecurity, Attor is a cyberespionage platform designed to exfiltrate valuable information from its victims.
