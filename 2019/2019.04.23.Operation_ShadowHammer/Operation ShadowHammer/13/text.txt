While investigating this case, we worked with several companies that had been abused in this wave of
supply chain attacks. Our joint investigation revealed that the attackers deployed several tools on an
attacked network, including a trojanized linker and a powerful backdoor packed with a recent version of
VMProtect.
Our analysis of the sophisticated backdoor (md5: 37e100dd8b2ad8b301b130c2bca3f1ea) that was
deployed by the attackers on the company’s internal network during the breach, revealed that it was an
updated version of the ShadowPad backdoor, which we reported on in 2017.
The ShadowPad backdoor used in these cases has a very high level of complexity, which makes it almost
impossible to reverse engineer:
