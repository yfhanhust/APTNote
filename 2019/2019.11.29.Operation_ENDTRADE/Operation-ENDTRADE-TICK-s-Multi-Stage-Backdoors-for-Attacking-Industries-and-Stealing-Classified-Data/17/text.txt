It mainly has six features:


Adds Autorun to the registry.


Gets MAC address and volume information and send back to the C&C.


Executes only during working hours (8:00AM-6:00PM, using kernel32.GetLocalTime API)


Uses AES encryption and base64 encoding method to encrypt the callback message.


Uses legitimate websites for the C&C server.


Detects antivirus products and processes.
