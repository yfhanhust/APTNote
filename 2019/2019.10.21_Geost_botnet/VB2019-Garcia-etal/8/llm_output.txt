4. BOTNET OPERATIONS
The main advantage of accessing the botmasters’ traffi c while they were using the HtBot network
was the possibility of a deep study of the attackers’ decisions and actions. The analysis helped to
identify a large botnet infrastructure, measure the size of the operation, and determine the goal of the
botnet. Based on the evidence found, the Geost operation seems to consist of a large number of APK
Android applications related to several topics, from banks and photo services, to fake social
networks. Once the applications are installed it seems that they may be able to interact directly with
the web services of fi ve banks in Eastern Europe. It seems that one of the goals of the botnet is to
access the personal information of the victims through their SMS messages, including those
messages sent by the banks. The rest of this section describes the actions of the botmasters and how
they helped identify each part of the Geost botnet. It is worth remembering that this was the traffi c
traversing our HtBot instance.
