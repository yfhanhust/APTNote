3. DISCOVERY
The Geost Android banking botnet was discovered as part of a larger malware analysis operation in
our laboratory. During an experiment in which a sample of the HtBot malware was executed [11], the
traffi c analysis revealed a very unusual communication pattern that stood out from the rest.
HtBot operates by converting its victims into unwilling private illegal Internet proxies. The infected
victims relay communications from the HtBot users to the Internet. HtBot is regarded as an
underground proxy network that is diffi cult for security analysts to tap, since its traffi c is continually
redirected to new victims. The users of the HtBot network pay the HtBot botmasters to provide them
with high-speed, semi-private communications for their operations.
