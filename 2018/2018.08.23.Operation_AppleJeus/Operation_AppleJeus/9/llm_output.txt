While working on the incident of the cryptocurrency company’s breach, we were curious about the legal

Celas LLC main homepage.
The website had a valid SSL certificate issued by Comodo CA. However, note that the certificate from this
webserver mentions “Domain Control Validated”, which is a weak security verification level for a webserver.
It does not mean validation of the identity of the website’s owner, nor of the actual existence of the
business. When certification authorities issue this kind of certificate they only check that the owner has a
certain control over the domain name, which can be abused in certain ways.
