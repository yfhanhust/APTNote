Conclusion
This activity shows us that TEMP.Zagros stays up-to-date with the latest code execution
and persistence mechanism techniques, and that they can quickly leverage these
techniques to update their malware. By combining multiple layers of obfuscation, they
deter the process of reverse engineering and also attempt to evade security products.
Users can protect themselves from such attacks by disabling Office macros in their
settings and also by being more vigilant when enabling macros (especially when
prompted) in documents, even if such documents are from seemingly trusted sources.
