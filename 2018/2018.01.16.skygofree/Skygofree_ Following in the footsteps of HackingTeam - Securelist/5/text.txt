‘device.db’ is a database used by the exploit. It contains two tables –
‘supported_devices’ and ‘device_address’. The first table contains 205
devices with some Linux properties; the second contains the specific
memory addresses associated with them that are needed for successful
exploitation. You can find a full list of targeted models in the Appendix.
