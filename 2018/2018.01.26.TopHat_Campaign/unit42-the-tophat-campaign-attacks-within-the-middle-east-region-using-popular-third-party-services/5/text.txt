Technique #1 – RTFs Leveraging Bit.ly
The first technique encountered included the use of malicious RTFs that made a HTTP request to the below URL which then
redirected to the below malicious site (note the intentional typo of “storage”):
|URL|Redirect|
|---|---|
|http://bit[.]ly/2y3XL3P|http://storgemydata[.]website/v.dat|
