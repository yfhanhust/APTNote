The StrongPity APT is a technically capable group operating
under the radar for several years. The group has quietly deployed
zero­day in the past, effectively spearphished targets, and
maintains a modular toolset. What is most interesting about this
group’s more recent activity however, is their focus on users of
encryption tools, peaking this summer. In particular, the focus was
on Italian and Belgian users, but the StrongPity watering holes
affected systems in far more locations than just those two. Adding
in their creative waterholing and poisoned installer tactics, we
describe the StrongPity APT as not only determined and well­
resourced, but fairly reckless and innovative as well.
