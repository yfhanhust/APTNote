Geolocations – WinRAR and TrueCrypt
Over the course of a little over a week, malware delivered from
winrar.it appeared on over 600 systems throughout Europe and
Northern Africa/Middle East. Likely, many more infections actually
occurred. Accordingly, the country with the overwhelming number
of detections was in Italy followed by Belgium and Algeria. The
top countries with StrongPity malware from the winrar.it site from
May 25th through the first few days of June are
Italy, Belgium, Algeria, Cote D’Ivoire, Morroco, France,
and Tunisia.

In a similar time­span, the over sixty visitors redirected from
winrar.be to ralrab.com for malicious file download were
overwhelmingly located in one country. The top countries directed
to StrongPity malware from the winrar.be site from May 25th
through the first few days of June are Belgium, Algeria, Morroco,
Netherlands, Canada, Cote D’Ivoire, and Tunisia.
