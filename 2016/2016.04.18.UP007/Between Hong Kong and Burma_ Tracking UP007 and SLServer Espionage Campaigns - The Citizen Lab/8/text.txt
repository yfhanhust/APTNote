UP007 Command and Control Infrastructure
The command and control server for the UP007 sample is hosted on Hong Kong provider New World Telecom at the IP
address 59.188.12[.]123. Passive DNS data from PassiveTotal indicates that the domain name yeaton.xicp[.]net
pointed to this IP from January 8 2016 to March 19 2016. In their recent report ASERT notes that the domain:
yeaton.xicp[.]net was used to advertise a Chinese VPN service in 2012. However, as ASERT explains given the long
period between the use of the domain for advertising and the recent threat activity the past uses of the domain may not
be related to the threat actors.
