7.1. Mexico: Politically Motivated Targeting?
In the case of Mexico, one target appears to be the journalist Rafael Cabrera, who recently reported on the Casa Blanca
controversy, a reported conflict of interest involving the President and First Lady of Mexico. On August 30, 2015 the journalist�
Cabrera tweeted that he had received suspicious messages purporting to come from Mexican television station UNO TV. His tweet
included screencaptures of the messages, which said that Mexico’s Presidency was considering defamation claims and
imprisonment of reporters related to the Casa Blanca report that Cabrera had worked on.
