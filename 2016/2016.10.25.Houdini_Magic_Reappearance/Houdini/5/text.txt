Final Thoughts:
The similarities in coding styles and features of the server, as well as languages and handles used by the author of the
malware, lead us to believe the beta builder is a version of Hworm which was created somewhere between the HTTP
version and the version used in the above outlined attack.
As this RAT can be found online in semi­public locations it is possible the malware is used by both surgical threat actors as
well as within casual compromises. The above attack is only one such campaign Unit 42 has discovered using the Delphi
versions of Hworm.
