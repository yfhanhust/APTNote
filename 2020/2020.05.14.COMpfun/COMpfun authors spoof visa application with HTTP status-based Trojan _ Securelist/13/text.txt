5/15/2020 COMpfun authors spoof visa application with HTTP status-based Trojan | Securelist
We saw innovative approaches from the COMpfun developers twice in 2019. First, they bypassed
TLS encrypted traffic via PRNG system function patching, and then we observed a unique
implementation of C2 communications using uncommon HTTP status codes.
The malware operators retained their focus on diplomatic entities and the choice of a visa-related
application – stored on a directory shared within the local network – as the initial infection vector
worked in their favor. The combination of a tailored approach to their targets and the ability to
generate and execute their ideas certainly makes the developers behind COMPFun a strong
offensive team.
