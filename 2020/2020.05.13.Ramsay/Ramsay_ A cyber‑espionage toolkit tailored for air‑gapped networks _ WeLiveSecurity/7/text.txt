infector, changing the structure of benign PE executable files held within removable and network shared
drives in order to embed malicious Ramsay artifacts triggered on host file execution.
The Spreader is highly aggressive in its propagation mechanism and any PE executables residing in the
targeted drives would be candidates for infection.
