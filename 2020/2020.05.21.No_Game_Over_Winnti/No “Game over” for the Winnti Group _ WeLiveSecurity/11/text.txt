PipeMon modules and installers are all signed with the same valid code-signing certificate that was likely stolen during a
previous campaign of the Winnti Group. The certificate’s owner revoked it as soon as they were notified of the issue.

We found on a sample sharing platform other tools signed with this certificate, such as HTRan, a connection bouncer, the
WinEggDrop port scanner, Netcat, and Mimikatz which may have been used by the attackers as well.
Furthermore, a custom AceHash build signed with a Wemade IO stolen certificate already mentioned in our previous
white paper and usually used by the Winnti Group was found on some machines compromised with PipeMon.
