1983 Rangoon Bombing
On October 9, 1983, three North Korean soldiers
attempted to assassinate then-South Korean President
Chun Doo Hwan while on a trip to Myanmar. A bomb at a
mausoleum the President was scheduled to visit
detonated early, killing 21 people, including the Korean
Foreign Minister and Deputy Prime Minister.
