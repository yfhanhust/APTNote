LAZARUS & WATERING-HOLE ATTACKS
On 3rd February 2017, researchers at badcyber.com released an article that detailed
a series of attacks directed at Polish financial institutions. The article is brief, but
states that "This is – by far – the most serious information security incident we have
seen in Poland" followed by a claim that over 20 commercial banks had been
confirmed as victims.
This report provides an outline of the attacks based on what was shared in the article,
and our own additional findings.
