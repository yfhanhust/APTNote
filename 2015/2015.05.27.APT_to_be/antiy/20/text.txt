Author of Cobalt Strike: Raphael Mudge

that creates software for red teams. He created Armitage for Metasploit, the Sleep
programming language, and the IRC client jIRCii. Previously, Raphael worked as a security
researcher for the US Air Force, a penetration tester, and he even invented a grammar
checker that was sold to Automattic. His work has appeared in Hakin9, USENIX ;login:, Dr.
Dobb’s Journal, on the cover of the Linux Journal, and the Fox sitcom Breaking In. Raphael
regularly speaks on security topics and provides red team support to many cyber defense
competitions.
