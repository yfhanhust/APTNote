About Qihoo 360
Qihoo 360 Technology Co. Ltd. (NYSE: QIHU) is a leading Internet and mobile
platform company in China, measured by user base. By December 2014, Qihoo
360 had about 509 million monthly active Internet users, and over 744 million
mobile users.
Recognizing security as a fundamental need of all Internet and mobile users,
Qihoo 360 built a large user base by offering comprehensive, effective and
user-friendly Internet and mobile security products. Qihoo 360 strives to provide
services that protect users' computers and mobile devices against malware and
malicious websites.
