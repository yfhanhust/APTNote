The fake WPL Summit 2023 page
On Aug. 8, 2023, Void Rabisu actors set up a website called wplsummit[.]com to attract
visitors of the legitimate wplsummit.org domain. The fake website (shown in Figure 1)
looked exactly like the legitimate one.
